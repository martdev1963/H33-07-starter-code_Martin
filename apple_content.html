Apple

Apple (Malus) is a genus of trees and shrubs in the rose family, Rosaceae. The cultivated apple (Malus domestica) originated in Central Asia and is now grown in temperate regions around the world.

Apple trees typically have white or pink blossoms in spring and produce crisp, edible fruits in a wide range of colors, flavors and sizes. Thousands of cultivars are grown for fresh eating, cooking and cider, with varieties differing in traits such as sweetness, acidity and harvest season.
