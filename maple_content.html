Maple

Maple (Acer) is a genus of trees and shrubs in the soapberry family, Sapindaceae. These trees are native to temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere and are known for their distinctive leaves, valuable wood, and sweet sap.

Maple trees often have opposite leaves with graceful lobes and brilliant red, orange, or yellow fall color. Species such as the sugar maple (Acer saccharum) are tapped to produce maple syrup, while many varieties are also grown for shade, ornamental landscaping, and timber.
